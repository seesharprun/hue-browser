Hue Browser is an app that helps you organize the Philips Hue lights in your home. It shows every light you own in one list, so you can give lights clear names, sort them into the right rooms, and check that each one is where you think it is.

You run Hue Browser on your own computer or home server. It talks to your Philips Hue bridges over your home network, and your information stays in your home.

## What you can do

Hue Browser focuses on keeping your lights organized rather than setting scenes or moods.

- See every light across all of your Philips Hue bridges in one place
- Rename lights, one at a time or many at once
- Move lights into the correct rooms
- Filter and group lights by room and by type
- Flash or switch a light to confirm which fixture you are looking at

## What you need

You need a few things before you start.

- A Philips Hue bridge connected to your home network
- A computer on that same network that can run Docker
- The button on top of your Philips Hue bridge, which you press once to grant access

## Get started

Read the [installation guide](/installation) to run the app, then [connect a bridge](/usage/connect-bridges). Managing your lights is still being built, and these guides grow as those features arrive.

## Appearance

Hue Browser and these docs follow your device's light or dark setting until you choose a theme with the button in the header. Your choice is remembered in this browser.
